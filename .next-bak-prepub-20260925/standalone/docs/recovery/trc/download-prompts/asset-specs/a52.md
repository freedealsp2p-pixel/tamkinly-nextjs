## Required Sections
### Page 1
- Title header with asset name
- Purpose statement
- How it works: brief psychoeducation on breath and nervous system
- Step-by-step breathing sequence:
  - Step 1: Inhale slowly for 5 seconds
  - Step 2: Hold briefly (1-2 seconds)
  - Step 3: Exhale slowly for 2 seconds (or longer)
  - Step 4: Pause naturally before next breath
- Breath timing indicator (visual rhythm guide)
- Practice rounds tracker (empty fields for 4-6 rounds)

## Safety Requirements
- When to use: Acute anxiety, hypervigilance, panic onset, need to slow heart rate
- When to stop: Dizziness, lightheadedness, chest tightness, feeling faint, tingling in extremities
- When not appropriate: Active respiratory distress, severe asthma attack, chest pain of cardiac origin
- When additional support needed: Breathing exercise triggers panic, cannot sustain rhythm, distress escalates
- Escalation language: "If breathing exercises make you feel worse, stop immediately. Return to natural breathing and try 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding instead."
