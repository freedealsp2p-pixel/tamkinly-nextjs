## 7. 🛑 Safety Section
- **When to use:** When feeling overwhelmed, anxious, dissociated, or disconnected from the present moment.
- **When to stop:** If distress increases, if dissociation worsens, if the person feels less safe, if physical symptoms arise (dizziness, nausea).
- **Not appropriate for:** Individuals in acute psychotic states where sensory focus may intensify hallucinations; individuals with severe PTSD where sensory engagement is contraindicated without therapist guidance.
- **Escalation:** If grounding fails and distress escalates, contact a mental health professional or crisis helpline immediately. Do not continue the exercise.
