## 2. Purpose

A comprehensive safety plan based on the Stanley & Brown Safety Planning Intervention, adapted for trauma recovery. This is a P0 critical document because it may be needed in crisis moments when digital access is unavailable. The user fills it in during a calm moment and keeps it accessible for emergencies.

## 3. Target User

Adult survivors of sexual trauma/harassment who:
- Experience acute distress episodes
- Need a concrete plan for crisis moments
- May not always have digital access
- Benefit from structured crisis preparation

## 4. When To Use

- Fill in during a calm, safe moment (NOT during crisis)
- Review weekly or when triggers increase
- Keep accessible (wallet, phone case, bedside)
- Use during escalating distress as a step-by-step guide

## 5. When Not To Use

From TRC Framework Section هـ:
- If currently in psychiatric emergency → call emergency services immediately
- If unable to engage cognitively (severe dissociation) → use grounding only
- This plan is NOT a substitute for professional crisis intervention

## 7. User Interaction

The user:
- Identifies their personal early warning signs (thoughts, feelings, body signals)
- Lists internal coping strategies (with checkboxes + custom entries)
- Names safe people and their contact information
- Specifies what to ask for from safe people
- Lists professional support contacts
- Describes environmental safety changes
- Fills in grounding reminder at bottom
