## 2. Purpose

A quick-reference card for the A52 (5-2) combat breathing technique adapted for trauma recovery. Designed for daily practice and acute stress regulation. Includes all 4 breathing patterns from TRC Framework Principle 8.

## 3. Target User

Adult survivors who need:
- Daily nervous system regulation
- Acute stress intervention
- Sleep preparation (Extended Exhale)
- Pre-therapy stabilization

## 4. When To Use

- Daily: 3-5 minutes morning and evening for chronic regulation
- Acute stress: during triggering moments
- Before sleep: Extended Exhale pattern for nightmares
- Before therapy: to enter window of tolerance

## 5. When Not To Use

From TRC Framework Section هـ:
- COPD/acute asthma → consult physician first
- Active panic attack where breathing focus increases anxiety → switch to grounding
- Recent chest injury → use grounding instead
- Unstable cardiac conditions → medical clearance required

Inhale 4 sec — Hold 4 sec — Exhale 4 sec — Hold 4 sec
[Visual: square diagram showing 4-4-4-4 pattern]
Repeat: 4-6 cycles
Best for: Panic, acute anxiety, emergency calm

PATTERN 3: Extended Exhale (Sleep/Nightmares) / الزفير الممتد (النوم/الكوابيس)
Inhale 4 sec — Exhale 6-8 sec (longer exhale)
[Visual: asymmetrical wave diagram showing 4-6+ pattern]
Repeat: 8-10 cycles
Best for: Before sleep, after nightmares, intrusive memories

PATTERN 4: "Calmmmm" (Emotional Pain) / "كممممم" (الألم العاطفي)
Inhale normally — Exhale while saying "calmmmm" slowly
Repeat: 5-8 times
Best for: Emotional pain, grief, shame spikes
