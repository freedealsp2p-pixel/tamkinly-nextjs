## 3. Therapeutic Purpose

### From Framework Principle 3:
Grounding is the **foundational** safety technique. It is the first tool taught and the one users return to most. It works by engaging the five senses to pull the nervous system out of trauma response (flashback/dissociation) and back into the present moment.

### Why a Printable Card (not just the web tool):

The digital grounding tool provides:
- Interactive step-by-step guidance
- Audio breathing circle
- DistressCheckIn after touch step
- Session persistence via localStorage
- Journey completion tracking

The printable pocket card provides what the digital tool **cannot**:
- **Offline access** — usable without phone/internet
- **Immediate availability** — no app launch, no loading
- **Physical anchoring** — the act of holding the card is itself grounding
- **No screen required** — important when screen is a trigger
- **Reusable indefinitely** — no session limits
- **Carryable** — wallet, pocket, bag, bedside

**This PDF is a Printable Companion, NOT a screenshot of the web page.**

## 4. Target User Profile

- Adult survivor of sexual trauma/harassment/assault
- Experiences flashbacks, intrusive memories, dissociation, or hyperarousal
- Arabic-speaking (this is the AR version)
- May be in a triggering environment where phone/screen is not accessible or is itself a trigger
- Needs immediate, reliable, self-directed regulation
- Has completed or is currently using the digital grounding tool at /recovery/trc/grounding
