## Safety Requirements
- When to use: Recognizing shame patterns, developing self-compassion, preparing for shame work in therapy, between sessions
- When to stop: Shame spirals deeper, self-compassion feels impossible/repulsive, increased self-harm urges, dissociation
- When not appropriate: Active crisis, active self-harm, severe depression where shame work reinforces hopelessness
- When additional support needed: Cannot access any self-compassion, shame leads to suicidal thinking, shame is connected to ongoing abuse
- Escalation language: "If shame is telling you that you don't deserve help — that is the shame lying. You deserve support. Reach out now."
