## Required Sections
### Page 1 — Introduction
- Title header with asset name
- Purpose: understanding your body's response is the first step to healing

### Page 2 — How Trauma Affects the Brain
- Amygdala: threat detection center (alarm system)
- Hippocampus: memory processing (why trauma memories feel present)
- Prefrontal cortex: reasoning and regulation (why logic fails in trigger)
- Visual: brain regions diagram (simple illustration)

### Page 3 — The Autonomic Nervous System
- Sympathetic nervous system: fight/flight activation
- Parasympathetic nervous system: rest/digest (and freeze)
- The vagus nerve and its role in regulation
- Visual: nervous system activation curve

### Page 4 — The 4F Trauma Responses
- Fight: what it looks like in the body and behavior
- Flight: what it looks like in the body and behavior
- Freeze: what it looks like in the body and behavior
- Fawn: what it looks like in the body and behavior
- Key: all are ADAPTIVE survival responses, not character flaws

### Page 5 — Body Memory and Somatic Storage
- How the body stores trauma (cellular, muscular, visceral)
- Why you may feel things "for no reason"
- Chronic pain, tension patterns, and trauma
- The concept of "the body keeps the score"

### Page 6 — The Window of Tolerance and Healing Pathways
- Window of tolerance: hyperarousal ↔ optimal zone ↔ hypoarousal
- Visual: window of tolerance diagram
- How to widen the window
- Brief overview of healing approaches: somatic, EMDR, CBT, mindfulness
- Safety section + emergency contacts
- Footer with tool ID and route

## Safety Requirements
- When to use: Psychoeducation, normalizing responses, understanding own patterns, preparing for therapy
- When to stop: Reading triggers overwhelming body memories, increased anxiety or dissociation while reading
- When not appropriate: Acute crisis, active psychosis, should not be used as substitute for therapeutic guidance
- When additional support needed: Information triggers hopelessness, reader identifies dangerous patterns without support
- Escalation language: "If reading about trauma responses feels overwhelming, pause here. You don't have to read it all at once. Try 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding and return when ready."
