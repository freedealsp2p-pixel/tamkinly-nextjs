## Purpose
- AR: إنشاء مكان آمن داخلي كأداة تنظيمية يمكن العودة إليه عند الشعور بالخطر أو الذعر
- EN: Creating an internal safe place as a regulatory resource to return to when feeling threatened or distressed

## Audience
Survivors of trauma seeking self-regulation tools. No clinical training required.

## Required Sections
### Page 1
- Title header with asset name
- Purpose statement
- Safety alert box: visualization can be triggering for some
- Instructions: How to choose and build your safe place
- 5 senses prompt: What do you see, hear, feel, smell, taste in your safe place?

### Page 2
- Writing fields for each of the 5 senses:
  - What I SEE in my safe place (empty field)
  - What I HEAR in my safe place (empty field)
  - What I FEEL (touch/temperature) in my safe place (empty field)
  - What I SMELL in my safe place (empty field)
  - What I TASTE in my safe place (empty field)
- Anchoring word or phrase field (empty)
- Emotion when in safe place field (empty)

### Page 3
- Safety section: when to stop visualization
- What to do if safe place becomes contaminated/distorted
- Returning to present — grounding reminder
- Emergency contacts template
- Footer with tool ID and route

## Safety Requirements
- When to use: Need for internal resource, between sessions, before exposure work, as daily practice
- When to stop: Visualization becomes distressing, intrusive images enter the safe place, increased dissociation
- When not appropriate: Active psychosis, unable to visualize, recent trauma with vivid intrusive imagery
- When additional support needed: Cannot establish a safe place after 3 attempts, safe place becomes contaminated, visualization triggers flashbacks
- Escalation language: "If your safe place feels unsafe or disturbing, gently open your eyes and use 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding to return to the present."
