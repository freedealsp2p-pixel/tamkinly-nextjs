## Required Sections
### Page 1
- Title header with asset name
- Purpose statement
- Quick reference table:

| Tool | When to Use | Time Needed | Key Instruction |
|------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding | Dissociated, flashback starting, overwhelmed | 2-5 min | Name 5 things you see, 4 you touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste |
| A52 Breathing | Anxious, hypervigilant, rapid heartbeat | 3-5 min | Inhale 5s, exhale 2s, repeat 4-6 rounds |
| Safe Place | Need internal resource, before difficult task | 5-10 min | Visualize your safe place through 5 senses |
| Body Scan | Tension, disconnection from body | 5-15 min | Scan from head to feet, notice without judgment |
| EFT Tapping | Specific named distress, stuck emotion | 10-20 min | Setup statement, tap 8 points, reassess SUDS |
| Thought Reframing | Stuck in negative thought loop | 10-15 min | Identify thought, examine evidence, reframe |
| Trigger Mapping | Post-incident, want to understand pattern | 10-15 min | Map trigger → body → emotion → behavior → response |

- Quick selection guide by arousal state:
  - HIGH arousal (hyper): Grounding → Breathing → Body Scan
  - LOW arousal (hypo): Movement → Sensory stimulation → Safe Place
  - STUCK (rumination): Thought Reframing → EFT Tapping
  - SHAME spiral: Shame Recovery → Self-compassion → Speaking shame

### Page 2
- Decision flowchart (text-based):
  - Start: "How distressed am I right now?"
  - SUDS 0-3: You may not need a tool right now
  - SUDS 4-6: Try Grounding or Breathing
  - SUDS 7-8: Use Grounding first, then Breathing or Body Scan
  - SUDS 9-10: Ground now. If not helping, reach out for support
- Escalation pathway:
  - Self-regulation not working after 10 minutes → Contact trusted person
  - Feeling unsafe to be alone → Call crisis line
  - Active suicidal thoughts → Call emergency services
- Emergency contacts template
- Footer with tool ID and route

## Safety Requirements
- When to use: Choosing the right tool, quick decision-making, reviewing available resources
- When to stop: This is a reference card — no emotional processing involved in reading it
- When not appropriate: Using as substitute for clinical guidance, reading during acute crisis (use tools directly instead)
- When additional support needed: None listed (reference card)
- Escalation language: "If nothing is working and you feel unsafe, you don't have to manage alone. Call for help now."
