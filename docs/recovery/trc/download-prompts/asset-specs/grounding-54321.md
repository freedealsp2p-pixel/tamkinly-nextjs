## Safety Requirements
- When to use: Feeling dissociated, overwhelmed, flashback beginning, anxiety escalating
- When to stop: If grounding increases distress, if unable to identify any sensory input, if panic worsens
- When not appropriate: Active psychosis, severe dissociative episode where reality testing is impaired
- When additional support needed: Distress continues after 2 full cycles, unable to complete any step
- Escalation language: "If you cannot feel safe after two rounds, reach out to someone you trust now."
