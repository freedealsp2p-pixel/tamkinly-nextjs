## 7. 🛑 Safety Section
- **When to use:** When creating a proactive plan for crisis moments. Best completed when calm and with support if possible. Should be reviewed and updated regularly.
- **When to stop:** If completing the plan causes acute distress or suicidal ideation, stop and contact a crisis line immediately. If any step triggers overwhelming emotion, skip to the next step or take a break.
- **Not appropriate for:** Individuals in active crisis (they need immediate support, not a planning tool); individuals who are intoxicated or in altered states; as a standalone intervention without any follow-up support.
- **Escalation:** If at any point during plan completion the person expresses intent to harm themselves, immediately connect them with crisis services (988 Lifeline, emergency room). The safety plan itself is an intervention — it should be reviewed with a clinician when possible.
