## 7. 🛑 Safety Section
- **When to use:** When experiencing moderate emotional distress that you want to reduce. When feeling anxious, frustrated, or upset about a specific identifiable issue. When wanting to work with a specific feeling rather than a vague sense of distress.
- **When to stop:** If overwhelming emotions or memories surface, if dissociation occurs, if distress increases after two complete rounds, if you feel worse than before starting.
- **Not appropriate for:** Individuals with active dissociative disorders (tapping on the body while focusing on distress may worsen dissociation); individuals in acute crisis; individuals with severe PTSD without therapist guidance; individuals who find physical self-touch triggering.
- **Escalation:** If EFT consistently triggers emotional flooding or dissociation, discontinue self-directed use and work with a therapist on emotional regulation skills. If acute distress or suicidal ideation emerges, contact crisis services immediately.
