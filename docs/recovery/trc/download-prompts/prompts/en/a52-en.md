## 7. 🛑 Safety Section
- **When to use:** When feeling anxious, overwhelmed, activated, or when breathing is shallow/rapid. Suitable for moment-to-moment regulation.
- **When to stop:** If distress increases, if dizziness or lightheadedness occurs, if breath-holding causes panic, if anxiety worsens after several cycles.
- **Not appropriate for:** Individuals with severe panic disorder where breath focus may trigger panic attacks; individuals with respiratory conditions who cannot comfortably extend inhale duration; individuals who find breath awareness traumatizing (trauma-sensitive considerations).
- **Escalation:** If breathing exercises consistently increase distress, discontinue use and consult a mental health professional. If acute panic or respiratory difficulty occurs, seek immediate medical attention.
