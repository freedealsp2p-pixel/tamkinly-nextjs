## 2. Purpose

A wallet/pocket-sized quick-reference card for the 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding technique. Designed for immediate use during trauma symptom activation — when the user cannot access the app. This is the most critical safety tool because it works offline, in any situation.

## 3. Target User

Adult survivors of sexual trauma/harassment who experience:
- Flashbacks or intrusive memories
- Panic symptoms
- Dissociative episodes
- Hyperarousal in triggering situations

## 4. When To Use

- During acute distress when the app is not accessible
- In triggering environments (crowds, medical settings, certain locations)
- As daily practice to build grounding habit
- Before therapy sessions to stabilize
- After nightmares to return to present

## 5. When Not To Use

From TRC Framework Section هـ:
- If person is in active psychiatric emergency (suicidal/self-harm) → call emergency services
- If dissociation is so severe that person cannot engage senses at all → seek immediate professional help
- If grounding increases distress (rare but possible) → stop and use breathing only

## 6. Therapeutic Structure

**Page 1 (Front):**

```
TRC — GROUNDING POCKET CARD / بطاقة التأريض

⚠️ If you are in danger right now, call emergency services.

5-4-3-3-2-1 SENSORY GROUNDING
تأريض 5-4-3-2-1 بالحواس

Step 1: Look — انظر
5 things you can SEE: ___ ___ ___ ___ ___

Step 2: Touch — المس
4 things you can TOUCH: ___ ___ ___ ___

Step 3: Listen — استمع
3 things you can HEAR: ___ ___ ___

Step 4: Smell — شمّ
2 things you can SMELL: ___ ___

Step 5: Taste — ذُق
1 thing you can TASTE: ___

"I am here. I am now. I am safe."
"أنا هنا. أنا الآن. أنا في أمان."
```

**Page 2 (Back):**

```
QUICK BREATHING / تنفس سريع
A52 Pattern: Inhale 5 sec — Hold 2 sec — Exhale 5 sec
Repeat 5-10 times

WHEN GROUNDING ISN'T ENOUGH / عندما لا يكفي التأريض:
• You are not failing — some moments are harder
• Try: cold water on wrists, feet firmly on floor
• If dissociation persists: name 5 blue objects aloud
• If panic persists: call someone safe

REMEMBER / تذكّر:
Your brain is stuck in survival mode. Grounding reminds it:
"The danger has ended. Now is safe."
دماغك عالق في وضع النجاة. التأريض يذكّره: "الخطر انتهى. الآن آمن."

HELP / مساعدة:
Therapist: _______________
Helpline: _______________
Emergency: _______________
```

## 7. User Interaction

The user:
- Counts and names sensory items (writes or says them)
- Practices breathing pattern
- Reads reassurance statements
- Fills in emergency contacts

## 8. Examples

No examples needed — this is a direct technique card.

## 9. Safety

- Trauma warning on front
- "If in danger → call emergency" prominently displayed
- Breathing backup if grounding insufficient
- Dissociation escalation protocol
- Emergency contact fields
- From Framework Principle 3: grounding is foundational, always available

## 10. Layout

- 2 pages (front + back)
- Credit card size when folded (approx 85 × 55 mm) OR A5 half-sheet
- Large, clear typography for use during distress
- High contrast (readable in low light)
- Color: TRC teal (#1F6F78) for headers

## 11. AR/EN Requirements

- Arabic first, English labels secondary
- Formal clinical Arabic
