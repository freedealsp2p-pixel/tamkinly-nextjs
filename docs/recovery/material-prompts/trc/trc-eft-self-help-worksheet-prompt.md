## 2. Purpose
A self-help reference worksheet for EFT (Emotional Freedom Techniques) tapping. Includes tapping point diagram, session structure, and tracking table. From Framework Worksheet 6 and EFT Specification. IMPORTANT: This material is clinical-review — must not be presented as evidence-based treatment.

## 3. Target User
Adult survivors seeking self-help regulation tools, who have completed Safety stage and feel connected to their body (no active dissociation).

## 4. When To Use
- When feeling emotionally activated but not dissociated
- After grounding to address residual emotional intensity
- As a self-regulation practice between therapy sessions

## 5. When Not To Use
From Framework Section هـ: ACTIVE DISSOCIATION → stop, redirect to grounding immediately. Also: if intensity increases after tapping → stop and ground. Max 3 rounds recommended.

## 6. Therapeutic Structure
Page 1: Trauma warning + clinical-review notice ("This is a self-help tool, not treatment") + dissociation screen ("Do you feel connected to your body?") + body diagram showing 9 tapping points with labels
Page 2: Setup phrase template + tapping sequence (KC→EB→SE→UE→UN→CH→CB→UA→TH) + SUDS before/after + session tracking table + grounding reset + referral

## 7. User Interaction
User identifies issue, rates SUDS before, follows tapping sequence, rates SUDS after, tracks sessions.

## 8. Examples
Example box: Setup phrase: "Even though I have [this anxiety about meetings], I deeply and completely accept myself." Reminder phrase: "[Anxiety about meetings]"

## 9. Safety
CLINICAL-REVIEW status. Dissociation screening mandatory. Stop criteria: intensity increases → stop + ground. Max 3 rounds. Grounding reset after session. Therapist referral. From EFT Specification.

## 10. Layout
2 pages A4. Body diagram with tapping points. Tapping sequence. SUDS scale. Session log.
